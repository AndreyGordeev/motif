How it works

Unlock the pack once inside the app — a one-time purchase, no subscription. Motif then hands you the icon shortcuts, wallpapers and widget art, and you apply them yourself with the built-in step-by-step guide.

iOS does not let one app restyle your home screen by itself, so applying a pack is something you do, guided by the app: each icon is added as a native “Open App” shortcut that opens its app directly, and wallpapers are set in Settings → Wallpaper. Motif never installs a configuration profile and never changes your device without you.

Privacy

No account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Packs and the catalog ship inside the app, and the purchase is verified on-device by the App Store. The optional weather widget shows local conditions using Apple’s built-in WeatherKit; your location answers that request and never reaches us. Full detail on the privacy page.
